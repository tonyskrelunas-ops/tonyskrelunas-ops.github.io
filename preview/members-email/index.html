Friends,

In January of 2021 a writer named Kaitlyn Benally interviewed me about why I had started a magazine. I told her it was to bestow knowledge, story, uplifting articles and community innovation on our people. I called it Tribal Prestige. The logo was that horse up there, drawn in a single line, two eagle feathers in its mane.

It is back, and this time it runs itself. Every morning it goes out and gathers good news from the world — not cheerful news, which is a different and emptier thing, but news of people building, restoring, healing and remembering. Then it sets my own writing beside what it found, the way I did by hand five years ago.

What is new is that it now reads in six editions, each one with its own engine reading its own sources, because a reader in Guatemala should not be handed a translated English headline. Spanish comes from Guatemala, Mexico, Peru and Spain. Japanese from IDEAS FOR GOOD, greenz and Sotokoto. Lithuanian from Bernardinai and the National Museum. There is an India edition, and as of today a Tibet edition.

Nothing changes about what you already get. When I write something, it comes to you the way it always has. What is new is that the magazine turns here every morning on its own, and once a week I will send you one letter with the best of what it found — this is the first of those. One a week, not one a day. If a week ever goes by and it is thin, I would rather send you nothing than send you filler.

You do not have to go anywhere to read it. Today’s is below.

The page as it stands this morning. The sun crosses it, the eagle goes over, and the messenger runs the ridge.

Thank you for reading all this time. It is the reason any of it got built.
