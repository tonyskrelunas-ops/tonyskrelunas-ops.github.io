They had already had the service. There was nothing to rush toward and nothing to fix. There was only this, arriving quiet and steady across an ocean: the songs were his to carry now.

He sat with it, and he felt no grief so much as weight — the good weight of being handed something. Here was the thing he had never quite let himself see. He had stood at this fire and named three threads with pride, and one of them he had touched the least. He could teach a canyon full of strangers to stop running from their own lives, and he had spent his own at a careful distance from the half of him that was now, suddenly, his to carry. There was a box of fifty years of songs in a green country he had never once set foot in. And there was no one left to carry it but him.
