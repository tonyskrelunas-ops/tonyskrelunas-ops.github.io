THE TOOL: THE DAINA PRACTICE
THE TOOL: THE DAINA PRACTICE

Origin:  Lithuanian dainos – the living library of a people.

Origin:  Lithuanian dainos – the living library of a people.

The Practice: Find a song that carries meaning for you. It doesn't have to be ancient. It doesn't have to be in another language. It just has to hold something true. Learn it. Not perfectly – just enough to carry it. Sing it. Not for anyone else. Sing it for yourself. Sing it to the trees. Sing it in the car. Sing it when you're afraid. Teach it to one other person. The song does not survive in a recording. It survives in a throat.

Why It Works: Singing releases endorphins and oxytocin, creating feelings of well-being and social bonding (Frontiers in Human Neuroscience). Vibration stimulates the vagus nerve. Songs carry memory more reliably than words because they engage multiple neural pathways – rhythm, melody, emotion, and language simultaneously.
