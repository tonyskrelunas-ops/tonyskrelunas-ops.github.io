At a council fire nobody performs. People arrive carrying something, they set it down in the middle where everyone can see it, and the fire is bigger for it. Then somebody else speaks.

That is what this is. Not a mailing list. Not a comment section. A place to say the thing the book brought up, or the thing you have been carrying this week, or simply where you are and what the weather is doing there.

I read every one of them. I answer more of them than you would expect.

This is the room the page is imitating. Everyone facing in. Nobody at the front.
