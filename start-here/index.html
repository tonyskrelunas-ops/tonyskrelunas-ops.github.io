Four doors. One destination: a body that can carry what matters across hard ground.

TribeAwaken works with three kinds of people — and one kind of organization. Each path leads to the same place. Pick the door that matches where you are right now.

Path 1 · The individual in alarm

I'm a leader or founder who's winning — and still waking up tight.

You're succeeding by every external metric and still running on alarm. The nervous-system work is where everything else begins. Tony's 1:1 coaching and wellness protocols are built for people who have tried everything else.

Path 2 · The institution in transition

I lead a community, organization, or nation ready to move from extraction to ownership.
