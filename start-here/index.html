Not sure where you fit? Start here.

Four doors. One destination: a body that can carry what matters across hard ground.

TribeAwaken works with three kinds of people — and one kind of organization. Each path leads to the same place. Pick the door that matches where you are right now.

Path 1 · The individual in alarm

I'm a leader or founder who's winning — and still waking up tight.

You're succeeding by every external metric and still running on alarm. The nervous-system work is where everything else begins. Tony's 1:1 coaching and wellness protocols are built for people who have tried everything else.

Explore Coaching & Wellness → Take the 90-second diagnostic first →

Path 2 · The institution in transition

I lead a community, organization, or nation ready to move from extraction to ownership.

You've seen the leakage — wealth, talent, health, culture — and you're ready to build something that keeps it home. Tony's consulting spans economic development, wellness villages, governance redesign, and regenerative master plans.

Explore Community Resilience →

Path 3 · The seeker of experience

I want to experience this work in person, with others carrying something real.

Retreats and gatherings — the containers where the practices come alive. The Canyon Within ran at 1440 Multiversity in August; the next gatherings are coming together now.

Explore the Retreat →

Path 4 · The reader

I came here through the books. I want to go deeper.

The Ancestral Watch series is where the principles live as story. If you've read Stone Breath or The Council Fire and want to know what comes next — or how the fiction connects to the practice — this is your door.

Visit AncestralWatch.com →
