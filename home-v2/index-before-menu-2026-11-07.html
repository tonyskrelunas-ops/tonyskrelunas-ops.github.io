01

I learned everything by lamplight, and by helping Masan.

If you have read this far, you are carrying something: a family pattern you want to end with you, a community deciding what to keep, a mind that has not been quiet in years. I was raised for that carry.

My great-grandparents raised me at a sheep camp on Black Mesa, in a hogan without electricity, where Masan taught me to speak only what I want built. In college I met an identity crisis and an inferiority crisis, came through on one discovery, that I could be who I was, and graduated Standard Bearer.

After thirty years in councils and boardrooms, I write the Ancestral Watch novels, publish the free Abundance Mindset Magazine, and lead sessions and retreats on the far messenger principles. For my doctorate in human performance and intergenerational healing I study the far messengers of the desert, the ocean, the high passes and the songlines, runners and navigators who, continents apart, arrived at the same skills: settling the breath and the nervous system under threat, lasting the distance, and reading their bodies and the world. All of it is for the one carrying something a long way. There is room.

Two kinds of resilience, one word.

The person, the family and the nervous system on one side. The place, its circle and the way it decides together on the other. Everything on this site stands in one of these two, and most of it stands in both.

02

Human Resilience

Intergenerational healing, long life, and the breath. A nervous system is learned before language and handed on, and it can be settled and handed on differently. My grandfather reached 103, and a long life was once common where I come from, so the second subject here is how to live that long and that well.
