Why this page exists

A fire is not an audience.

If you want a place to start:

  • 01The line that stopped you — and why it stopped you.
  • 02Something you are carrying right now.
  • 03Where you're reading from, and what the land looks like there.

At a council fire nobody performs. People arrive carrying something, they set it down in the middle where everyone can see it, and the fire is bigger for it. Then somebody else speaks.

That is what this is. Not a mailing list. Not a comment section. A place to say the thing the book brought up, or the thing you are carrying this week, or simply where you are and what the weather is doing there.

I read every one of them. I answer more of them than you would expect.
