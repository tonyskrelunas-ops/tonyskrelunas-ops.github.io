Who is writing

Tony Skrelūnas

Diné and Lithuanian. Raised on Black Mesa by his great-grandmother, Masan, who taught with the day rather than with lectures.

He has spent his working life on tribal economies — nation-building, honorable trade, land and water — and the last decade convening the cooks, singers, runners and elders who still carry the practice. He holds a PhD, serves as faculty at 1440 Multiversity, and writes the four-book Ancestral Watch series.

These essays are the field notes. He builds the walls himself, too.
