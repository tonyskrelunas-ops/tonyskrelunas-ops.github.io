The Writing · since 2019

A few questions I have not let go of.

I have been writing this down since 2019, mostly early, before anybody needed anything from me.

It is not a blog. I do not post to keep up. I write when a question will not leave me alone, and then I keep writing about that same question for years, from different sides, until I understand it a little better than I did.

So these are grouped by the question they belong to, not by the month they went up. Inside each one they run oldest first, because the interesting part is watching a thought change its mind.

Some of it was written by other hands — my nieces, my family, people I sat down with. Those say so.

Intergenerational Healing

My great-grandmother raised me in a hogan on Big Mountain where almost nothing came as explanation, and what I took in there, the calm of that house, the watched word, the joke told before the hard thinking, turned out to be the method I have been teaching ever since, at Twin Arrows, at 1440, and in the meditations I have been recording since Labor Day. These are the newest pieces, and they circle one question: how a nervous system is handed down a family line, and how a person, then a home, then a community gets it settled again.

Ancestral Economics

The oldest economies on earth were not built for shareholder wealth. They were built for honor, for kinship, and for the great-grandchildren. The question underneath all of these is the same one: where does the money go after it leaves your hand, and what would it take to keep it home. The autumn of 2020 sits in the middle of this stream, when the coal plants were closing and the roads were shut, and I went looking for the people who were still open for business.
