# TribeAwaken — First Facebook Post (Council Fire → The Canyon Within)

In *The Council Fire*, when the modern world begins to buckle, twelve keepers of the oldest knowledge are drawn together — not to fight the collapse, but to remember the way through it.

I didn't write that as fantasy. I wrote it as a map.

Look around. Heatwaves. Storms that don't behave. Food systems straining. Trust in institutions thinning. And your body feels every headline — the nervous system held *on* with no lion in the grass. We've become a people running from something we can no longer name.

Every culture that ever faced a breaking world did the same two things. They kept a **council fire** — a circle where the elders, the mothers, and the daughters remembered what actually lasts. And they raised **far messengers** — the ones trained to carry what mattered across hard ground, and arrive whole.

That knowledge didn't die. It went dormant. And it can be woken.

This August, I'm gathering a small circle — just eighteen — for three days of exactly this. Breath. Story. Sound healing. Food as medicine. The tools three cultures kept alive when the world told them to forget. Grandmother, mother, daughter, moving together. We call it **The Canyon Within.**

You come down carrying something heavy. You go home lighter, steadier, more yourself.

The world is loud and fast right now. This is three days of remembering how to be well inside it.
