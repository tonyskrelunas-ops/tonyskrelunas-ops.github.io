# LinkedIn Ad — Campaign Manager Setup Spec

## Prerequisite
- LinkedIn ads require a **Company Page** (can't run from a personal profile).
- Create at: linkedin.com → "For Business" → "Create a Company Page" (free).

## Campaign
- Objective: **Website visits**
- Ad format: **Single image ad**
- Landing URL: https://www.tribeawaken.com/workshop
- CTA button: **Learn More** (or **Register**)

## Targeting (this is the part FB couldn't do)
- **Locations:** San Francisco Bay Area · San Jose · Santa Cruz County
  - (Do NOT add any other metros — that alone keeps it out of Indian Country.)
- **Job seniority:** Manager · Director · VP · CXO · Owner · Partner
- **Job functions:** Engineering · Entrepreneurship · Operations · Product · Business Development
- **Company size:** 11+ employees
- **(Optional interests):** Mindfulness · Leadership development · Wellness
- Estimated audience should land ~50k–200k. If it's under 20k, drop the "interests" filter.

## Budget
- Minimum ~$10/day. Recommended: **$15–20/day.**
- Run window: now → ~Aug 25 (stop before the retreat fills).
- Set a **total budget cap** (~$300) so it can't run away.

## Creative
**Image:** 1200×627. Use a clean redwood / calm image (NOT the tribal-art pieces).
**Intro text (first ~150 chars show before "see more"):**
> Peak performance and burnout look identical from the outside. Three days in the redwoods to reset your nervous system — for the people who run hard.

**Headline (~70 char max):**
> The Canyon Within — a 3-day reset for high performers

**Description:**
> Aug 28–30 · 1440 Multiversity, Scotts Valley · 18 seats · day passes available
