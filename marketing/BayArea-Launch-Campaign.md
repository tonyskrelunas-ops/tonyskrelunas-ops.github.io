1. **Local partnerships (best ROI, low cost)** — yoga/meditation studios, wellness centers, the ISKCON temple community, Indian cultural orgs, longevity/biohacking meetups, 1440 alumni, corporate-wellness programs at tech firms. Co-host intro evenings.
2. **Meta ads (Instagram + Facebook)** — geo-target SF / San Jose / Silicon Valley / LA. Interests: meditation, Blue Zones, Ayurveda, yoga, personal development, Esalen, 1440, Rancho La Puerta, Huberman; + South Asian affinity. **Retarget site visitors** (highest converting).

4. **Podcasts & press** — leverage your Resonate interview; pitch "Navajo-Lithuanian wisdom for Silicon Valley burnout" to Bay Area wellness & founder podcasts.
5. **Local listings** — Eventbrite, Meetup, wellness calendars.
6. **Email** — your list + weekly wisdom nurture → workshop/coaching.

## 5. Launch sequence
- **Week 0 (setup):** workshop landing page live at tribeawaken.com/workshop · Meta Pixel + analytics installed · email tool connected.
- **Week 1 (warm):** organic teasers (your story, a Rancho reel, the far-messenger) · email announce to your list.
- **Week 2 (open):** Meta ads live (awareness → free Stone Breath + workshop) · begin partnership outreach.
- **Week 3–4 (convert):** retargeting ads (visitors → register) · testimonials · podcast pushes.
- **Ongoing:** weekly wisdom email nurtures the list toward the workshop, books, and coaching.

## 6. Budget tiers
- **Lean — $300–500/mo:** organic + partnerships + ~$10/day Meta *retargeting only*.
- **Growth — $1.5–3k/mo:** Meta prospecting + retargeting + one local podcast/micro-influencer.
