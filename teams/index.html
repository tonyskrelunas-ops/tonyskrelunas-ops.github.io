For teams · The Far Messenger Program

Awaken the Far Messenger in your team.

A 2.5–3 day immersive that lifts your people to a higher frequency — clear, resilient, and fully alive — and sends each leader home carrying what matters most. We bring the whole team, and we show you the rise.

Every person on your team already carries a Far Messenger — the one who can hold what matters and move it across hard ground, and arrive whole. Our work is not to fix anyone. Nothing is broken. It is to raise the frequency until that messenger wakes.

We are entering harder terrain than most organizations have ever had to cross — accelerating technology, systems under strain, a world on edge. The answer is not to grip tighter. It is to become the kind of people, and teams, who can carry what matters across the hardest ground and arrive whole. That capacity has a name — Far Messenger resilience — and it can be built.

The best teams don't run on pressure — they run on coherence. A nervous system that has remembered how to settle makes clearer decisions, hears people better, and creates from abundance instead of from scarcity. That state is not a personality trait. It is trainable, it is measurable, and it is the quiet engine under every good decision, every person who chooses to stay, and every thing your team builds that lasts.

The arc — 2.5 to 3 days

Day 1 — Arrive

Return to stillness

We begin outdoors, on the land, with a land acknowledgement — honoring the ground and the people whose home it is. Everyone settles into nature and into calm, dropping to a clear, open baseline: the ground all real change is built on.

Day 2 — Awaken

Raise the frequency

Breath, sound, movement, food, and council — the living tools three cultures kept alive, delivered by our team, lifting the whole group together.

Day 3 — Carry

Take the frequency home

Integration + a 30/60/90 path so each leader carries the new baseline into the real work on Monday.

The signature journey

The Far Messenger Path

Underneath every practice runs one facilitated journey. We guide each person to name what they are here to carry — the gift, the message, the work only they can move across hard ground — and to build the resilience to carry it in abundance. Through custom hikes, time with elders, writing, and council, your people don't just recover. They awaken, and begin to build their own Far Messenger.

Proven in the field

This is not theory.

For ten years, Tony led inter-tribal gatherings — bringing together cooks, sauna leaders, running coaches, dancers, singers, and facilitators to do the deep work of intergenerational healing. He has since brought his team to deliver at premier wellness centers — Rancho La Puerta (three times) and Sedona — and is now organizing at 1440 Multiversity, where he is faculty. The Far Messenger Program brings that proven model — the full team, the real transformation — to organizations and companies.

Your guide

Tony Skrelūnas is Lithuanian and Diné — raised in a hogan on Big Mountain, learning by lamplight after the chores were done, reading the philosophy of Japan by the same small flame, a discipline he would take as his own. He failed out of college, came back, and finished at the top of his class. Today he is a PhD candidate in Sustainable Development at Prescott College, where his doctoral research studies exactly what this work delivers: how the ancestral tools of breath, ceremony, and community hold up as a high-performance system for the modern world. He is the first person of Diné and Lithuanian descent inducted into Northern Arizona University's W. A. Franke College of Business Hall of Fame.

He knows how to move real institutions. A three-time executive and systems architect, he led the governance restructuring of a still-traditional nation — returning real ownership and self-determination to its local communities — advises companies and communities on four continents, and serves as development advisor to a $40M integrated health-and-wellness development. For a decade he convened Indigenous elders across the Americas in the old way — the living model this program is built from. He is board chair of Red Road Journey, faculty at 1440 Multiversity, a runner and aspiring silversmith, and the author of the Ancestral Watch series.

Across all of it runs one conviction: the tools were never lost, only set down — and a person, a team, or a whole people can remember their power and rise into it. Money is only ever one instrument in that work; the real aim is wholeness that lasts for generations. For each engagement, Tony assembles and leads the practitioner team.
