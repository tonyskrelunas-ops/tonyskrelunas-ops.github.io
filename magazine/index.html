How this page is made

Early each morning the engine underneath this page reads fifteen publications for this edition — Good News Network, Positive News, Reasons to be Cheerful, Greater Good, iRunFar, Trail Runner, Canadian Running, Western Horseman, Hobby Farms, Modern Farmer, Civil Eats, Audubon, Mongabay, Atlas Obscura and Smithsonian — and keeps only what passes four tests: nothing violent or frightening, no party politics, on the subjects this magazine is about, and something actually built, healed, opened, won or celebrated. It stores a headline, a line and the link, and you read the piece where it was written. My own pieces from the journal sit beside what it found, the way I set them by hand in 2021. Some mornings are thin because nothing cleared all four tests, and I leave them thin.
