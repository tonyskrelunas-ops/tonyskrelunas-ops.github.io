New · Yes! We Rise · Episode 62 · November 2026

Stone Breath: Ancestral Practices for Calm, Courage, and Healing

Christine Gyovai welcomes Tony back to Yes! We Rise, five years after their first conversation. He tells the story of his great-great-grandfather Sorefoot, a celebrated runner of the late 1800s, and of the breathing and the training of the mind that let runners and knowledge carriers cross deserts, plains and mountains without panic.

Then he walks through the stone breath, step by step, and talks about why nobody learns or leads well in fight or flight, about choosing abundance over ego, gratitude at the table, stepping back from bad news, and finding the humor when the well runs dry.

The stone breath, as he gives it on the show

Picture a small pebble on your tongue. Let the tongue go soft, then the mouth, the shoulders and the temples. Breathe in for about four. Hold. Breathe out longer, six to eight. Hold for four.
